        Builds an oxRNA2 structure of an RNA from its sequence and (pseudoknotted) dot-bracket structure entirely in your browser
        (rigidRNA compiled to WebAssembly, in a background worker): rigid base-pair nodes per stem, de novo layout of the stems,
        Levenberg-Marquardt relaxation and export. With Electrostatics the stems also feel oxRNA2's salt-dependent Debye-Hückel repulsion
        between their backbone charges, so the stem spacing follows the salt concentration. The result opens as a normal system in the viewer.
